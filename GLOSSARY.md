# Eain Website

The public website and editorial CMS of Eain, a fictional Bangkok architecture and interior design studio.
Eain, its people, and its work are invented for a portfolio demo.

## The practice

**Studio**:
Eain, the fictional practice itself: a small Bangkok studio offering architectural and interior design services.
The Studio designs only; it does not provide licensed engineering, construction, or construction management.
_Avoid_: Company, firm, agency, business

**Service**:
A design service the Studio offers, described by its Spaces and its Scope, such as Residential Interior Design or Architectural Design.
_Avoid_: Discipline, offering, capability

**Spaces**:
The kinds of space a Service covers, such as houses, condominiums, or cafes.
Spaces describe a Service; a Typology classifies a Project.
_Avoid_: Sectors, building types

**Scope**:
The work a Service includes, such as space planning, 3D visualization, or design documentation.
_Avoid_: Deliverables, inclusions, features

**Team Member**:
A person shown publicly as part of the Studio.
A Team Member is not necessarily a CMS User.
_Avoid_: Staff, employee, profile

**Position**:
A Team Member's job title at the Studio, such as Founding Partner.
Not to be confused with the role a Credit names on a single Project.
_Avoid_: Title, job, role

## Portfolio

**Project**:
A piece of the Studio's work presented in the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Work, case study, portfolio item

**Typology**:
The kind of building or space a Project is: Residential, Commercial, or Workplace; each Project has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Category, type, sector

**Concept**:
A Project the Studio designed that has not been built.
_Avoid_: Unbuilt, proposal

**Credit**:
A Team Member named on a Project together with their role on it, such as Project architect.
_Avoid_: Contributor, team

## Journal

**Article**:
A piece of editorial writing published by the Studio, listed under the Journal.
_Avoid_: Post, blog post, insight, journal entry

**Byline**:
The Team Members credited as having written an Article, independent of which CMS User entered it.
_Avoid_: Author, writer

**Topic**:
The subject an Article belongs to, such as Material, Process, or Studio news; each Article has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Category, tag

**Publication date**:
The date readers see on an Article, set by an Editor; republishing an Article does not change it.
_Avoid_: Published at, post date

## Site

**Studio page**:
The page that presents the Studio, its approach, and its Team Members.
_Avoid_: About page, About us

**Page**:
A standalone page outside the structured content, such as Privacy.
_Avoid_: Landing page, custom page

**Block**:
One unit in the body of a Project, Article, or Page, such as text, an image, an image pair, a gallery, or a quote.
_Avoid_: Component, module, widget

**Drawing**:
A Block that shows a plan, section, or elevation uncropped, presented as a drawing rather than a photograph.
_Avoid_: Plan, diagram, technical image

**Section**:
One of the predefined parts an Editor arranges on the Home page or the Studio page.
_Avoid_: Block, slice, module

**Site Settings**:
The site-wide content an Editor manages in one place: the Studio's contact details, social links, header and footer navigation, Demo Notice text, and default search and sharing text.
_Avoid_: Global, config

**Demo Notice**:
The site-wide statement that the Studio and everything shown on the site are fictional.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer

## Visitors

**Inquiry**:
A message a visitor sends to the Studio through the contact form.
The Studio is fictional, but the visitor and their details are real.
_Avoid_: Lead, message, contact, submission

## Editorial

**CMS User**:
A person who signs in to the CMS to manage content.
_Avoid_: Admin, staff, account

**Super Admin**:
A CMS User with full control of the CMS, including CMS Users and their roles.

**Editor**:
A CMS User who can publish any content and change Site Settings.
_Avoid_: Publisher, admin

**Author**:
A CMS User who drafts and edits their own Articles but cannot publish them.
Not to be confused with an Article's Byline.
_Avoid_: Writer, contributor
