Eaves is a fictional studio, but you are real, so this page explains what happens to your details on this site.
The site is a portfolio demo run by its developer.

## What the contact form collects

When you send an Inquiry, the site stores what you enter:

- Your name
- Your email address
- The kind of inquiry you chose
- Your message
- That you gave your consent

Nothing is collected unless you submit the form.

## How it is used

Your Inquiry can be read by the people who manage this site's content.
Nobody receives an automatic email when you send it, and you will not be sent marketing.
Your details are not sold or shared for advertising.

## How long it is kept

Inquiries are deleted automatically after 90 days.
Database backups are kept for 30 days, so a deleted Inquiry can remain in a backup for up to 30 more days.

## Your IP address

Your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated messages from one visitor.
It is held in memory for about ten minutes, and it is not stored with your Inquiry.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a visitor is not an automated program, and the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits.
Cloudflare processes your connection in order to provide those services.

## Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data.
To do that, write to [hello@eaves.layaungphyo.dev](mailto:hello@eaves.layaungphyo.dev).
