Eaves does not exist.
It is a fictional Bangkok design studio, invented so that this website and its content management system could be built as a portfolio project.

## What is invented

The Studio, its Team Members, its Projects and its Articles are all fictional.
Every image on this site is AI-generated, and none shows a real building, interior or person.
The contact details are invented too, although the contact form works and a real person reads it.

The Studio holds no professional licences.
Nothing on this site is an offer of architectural, engineering or construction services.

## What is real

The website and its editorial system are real, and were built to work the way a real studio's would.
Editors can change every page, the navigation and the site-wide text without touching any code, and what they publish appears within seconds.

The source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/FreddieTheObserver/eaves).
A screen recording of the editing experience will be added to this page before launch.
