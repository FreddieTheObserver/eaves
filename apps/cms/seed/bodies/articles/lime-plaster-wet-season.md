A wall in Bangkok spends a good part of the year damp.
Cement render holds that damp in, and paint on top of it blisters and lifts.

Lime behaves differently.
It lets moisture pass through and leave again, so a wall can dry out when the rain stops.

## What it asks of you

Lime is slower to apply and easier to get wrong.
It needs to be kept damp as it cures, and it will mark if someone leans a ladder against it.
It also changes colour very slightly over the years.

We have come to think of that as a point in its favour.
In the shophouse in Talat Noi, the old walls were patched in lime so that the repairs could age along with everything around them.
