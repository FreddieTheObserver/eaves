Before a house has rooms, we draw where its shadow falls at noon in April.
It is a simple drawing, and most of the plan comes from it.

@image article-shade-diagram.jpg | A hand-drawn study of a roof's shadow across a site

## Starting from the roof

In this climate, the roof is the most important thing a building does.
A deep overhang keeps sun off the walls, and it lets windows stay open when it rains.
Once you know where the shade is, you know where people will want to sit.

The rooms then arrange themselves around those places.
It sounds backwards, but we have rarely regretted it.

The Courtyard House in Ari began this way, and so did the Hillside House, which remains a Concept.
