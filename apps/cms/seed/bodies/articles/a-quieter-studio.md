Some years ago we were asked whether we wanted to grow, and we took the question seriously.
We decided that we did not.

## What staying small means

We take on a few projects each year, and the people who draw them are the people you meet.
It means saying no quite often, which is uncomfortable but gets easier.

It also means our days are mostly spent drawing, and looking at samples, and arguing about door handles.
We would like to keep it that way.
