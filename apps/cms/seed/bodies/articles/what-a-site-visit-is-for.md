We go to a site to measure it.
We stay because of what the measurements leave out.

@image article-site-visit-street.jpg | A narrow street in the late afternoon, with a shopfront half shuttered

An afternoon on a street shows you where the shade falls and who stops in it.
It tells you when deliveries arrive, how loud the traffic gets and which corner fills with water when it rains.

## Sit down for a while

For the coffee bar in Ari, we spent three mornings at the corner before drawing anything.
That was how we learned that nearly everyone arrives from the same direction.
For the noodle counter in Yaowarat, we watched the cooks work through a whole service.

None of this needs special equipment.
It only needs a stool and a bit of patience.
