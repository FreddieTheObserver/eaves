The Studio designs and does not build.
We hold no professional licence, and we do not provide engineering, construction or management of a site.

That makes the engineers and contractors a client appoints the most important people we meet.
Our drawings are only useful if they can be read and questioned by the people working from them.

## What design coordination means here

We share drawings early, and we expect them to come back marked up.
We answer questions about the design, and we change it when an engineer shows us a better way.
Decisions about structure and safety belong to the people qualified to make them.

The Noodle Counter in Yaowarat is being built this way now, by a contractor the owner chose.
