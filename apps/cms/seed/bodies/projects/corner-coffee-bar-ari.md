## One counter, one bench

A single terrazzo counter runs the length of the room, so that people ordering, waiting and leaving all move in one direction.
Along the shaded side, a timber bench follows the wall and turns out onto the pavement.

@pair corner-coffee-bar-ari-bench.jpg | corner-coffee-bar-ari-facade.jpg

The shutters fold all the way back in the cool months.
When they are closed, a band of high glass keeps the room bright without letting the sun in.
