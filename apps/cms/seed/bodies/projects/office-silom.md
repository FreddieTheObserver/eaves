The team needed two very different kinds of day: long stretches of concentration, and short bursts of working things out together.
A single open floor would have served neither.

## Quiet up, loud down

The upper floor is for quiet work, with small rooms, thick curtains and desks turned away from one another.
The lower floor is for talking, with long shared tables, a pantry and rooms of different sizes.
People move between them according to the work in front of them.

@gallery office-silom-desks.jpg | office-silom-phone-rooms.jpg | office-silom-pantry.jpg

Daylight comes first, with electric light kept low and warm so that the rooms feel the same at the end of the day as they did at the start.
