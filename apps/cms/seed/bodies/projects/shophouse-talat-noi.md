The shophouse is four metres wide and twenty deep, and for decades its middle rooms had no daylight at all.
The owners wanted to live in it without losing what made it worth keeping.

@image shophouse-talat-noi-lightwell.jpg | The new lightwell, seen from the second floor

## Cutting a hole through the middle

The main move is a lightwell cut through all four floors.
It brings daylight into the centre of the plan, and warm air rises through it and leaves at the top.
The old walls stayed where they were, patched in lime so that they can keep drying out after each wet season.

@pair shophouse-talat-noi-front-room.jpg | shophouse-talat-noi-upper-floor.jpg

The ground floor keeps its folding shutters, and still opens to the street in the morning.
