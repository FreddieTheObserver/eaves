## One roof down the hill

A single long roof follows the slope, with the rooms stepping down beneath it.
Its deep overhang shades a terrace along the whole of the south side, and the roof collects rain into a tank at the lowest point.

The floor steps with the ground, so that very little of the hillside needs to be moved.
