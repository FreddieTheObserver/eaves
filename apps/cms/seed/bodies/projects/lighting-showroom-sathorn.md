Most showrooms are too bright to show you anything about a lamp.
This one is kept deliberately dim, so that each fitting can be seen doing its work.

@image lighting-showroom-sathorn-gallery.jpg | The main room with the display lights lowered

## A dark room, carefully

Walls and ceilings are finished in a deep, matt plaster that absorbs stray light.
Fittings hang in small groups, each on its own dimmer, and visitors can bring a light up or down themselves.

@pair lighting-showroom-sathorn-samples.jpg | lighting-showroom-sathorn-stairwell.jpg

A table at the back holds samples of timber, stone and fabric, so that a lamp can be tried against the materials of a real room.
