The plot in Ari is long, narrow and hemmed in by taller neighbours.
Rather than fill it, the design pulls the house back around a planted court, so that every main room has a window onto something green.

## A roof that does the work

The roof reaches a full metre and a half past the walls on the western side.
It keeps the afternoon sun off the brickwork and lets the family leave the windows open through a downpour.
Air drawn across the court moves through the living room and out through high vents, and for much of the year the rooms need little else.

@pair courtyard-house-ari-stair.jpg | courtyard-house-ari-kitchen.jpg

Inside, the palette is short: lime-washed walls, oiled timber and a terrazzo floor poured in one colour.
The stair is the only piece that asks to be looked at.
