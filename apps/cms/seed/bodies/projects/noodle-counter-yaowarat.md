This old shopfront is being reworked into a counter of twelve stools.
Work is under way, built by a contractor the owner appointed, and these images show the design rather than the finished room.

## Keeping the street in the room

The kitchen stays at the front, where it has always been.
Steam and noise are part of what brings people in, so the counter wraps around the cooks instead of hiding them.

@gallery noodle-counter-yaowarat-stools.jpg | noodle-counter-yaowarat-shutters.jpg

The stainless steel is plain and can be scrubbed.
The timber is kept to the places where a hand or an elbow rests.
