This apartment had a very good view and a plan that ignored it.
Two small bedrooms took up the whole river front, and the living room faced a corridor.

The new plan turns that around.
The living space now runs the full width of the river side, and the rooms that need privacy sit behind it.

## One long piece of joinery

A single run of timber joinery travels the length of the apartment.
It holds the books, the kitchen, the wardrobes and the many things a couple accumulates over twenty years.
With everything inside it, the rest of the rooms can stay almost empty.
