# Fictional content policy

Eaves is a fictional studio built as a portfolio demo, so nothing on the site may present real work, real people, or real contact details as the Studio's own.
Project imagery and Team Member portraits are AI-generated or abstract, never stock photos of real buildings and interiors, because a licensed photo of a real space still credits someone else's built work to a fictional firm.
Image search shows pictures without the page around them, so the Demo Notice says every image is AI-generated, alt text never calls an image a photograph, and original image files carry the IPTC marker for AI-generated media.
The Studio is design-only: no copy, Service, or Project may imply licensed engineering, construction, or construction-management services, so a Completed or Under construction Project is built by others to the Studio's design, and that scope changes only by a deliberate decision.
Nothing claims a professional licence either, because Thailand's Architect Act makes architecture and interior architecture regulated professions: no "Registered Architect" titles, licence numbers, or Architect Council membership; Positions and Credit roles use designer titles; design coordination means working with engineers and contractors the client appoints; and the About this demo page says the Studio holds no professional licences.
Contact details are invented (no real street address or phone number, and email only on a domain we own), and a Demo Notice appears in the site footer and on an About this demo page.
Invented names can collide with real businesses, so Projects have no client field, Credits name only Team Members, and any invented business name, the Studio's included, is checked against real Bangkok practices first; "Chaan" was rejected as a studio name because a real Bangkok practice already uses it.
Search engines may index the site, so the demo can be found and its SEO work checked in real search results; blocking indexing in production was considered and rejected.
To keep those results honest, page descriptions say up front that the Studio is fictional, and structured data never describes the Studio as a local business with an address, phone number, or opening hours.

## Consequences

Visitors are real even though the Studio is not, so an Inquiry is treated as real personal data under Thailand's PDPA: it requires consent, triggers no automatic emails sent in the Studio's name, and is deleted after 90 days.
